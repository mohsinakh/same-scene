A QUICK GUIDE

A little setup.
A whole movie night.

Start with a movie file

On your Mac in Firefox, start a room, copy the invite, and choose a local movie file. MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio is a good first choice. Use the player’s play, pause, and seek controls. The file stays on your device; live picture and sound go to your guest. Files your browser cannot decode need VLC + screen sharing instead.

Share Firefox or VLC with movie audio

  1. Install the free BlackHole 2ch driver.
  2. For VLC, open Preferences → Audio and choose BlackHole 2ch as VLC’s audio output. Keep your Mac’s normal output on your headphones so your guest’s voice does not get sent back.
  3. In this app, click “Find devices”, grant input access, then choose BlackHole 2ch under “Movie audio for screen sharing”. Keep your real microphone selected under “Your microphone”.
  4. Click “Share a window”, select the movie window, and allow screen recording for Firefox in macOS System Settings if requested.
  5. To hear VLC locally too, create a Multi-Output Device with BlackHole + your headphones and select it in VLC only. Keep the system output on headphones. BlackHole setup guide ↗

Sharing movie audio from another Firefox tab needs application-specific routing (for example OBS application audio capture into BlackHole). Sending all Mac sound to BlackHole also sends your guest’s voice back, causing echo. The local-file option avoids this setup.

Your guest

They open the invite in Chrome on Windows or Android and tap “Join movie night”. They can turn their microphone and camera on independently. Keep the browser open and the phone unlocked; backgrounding Chrome may interrupt playback or the call. On a phone, use landscape fullscreen for the movie.

Privacy & connection

One host and one guest are supported in this first version. Anyone with the full invite can join, so share it privately. Roles are for a trusted pair, not account authentication. Create a new room to get a new invite. Media is encrypted in transit using WebRTC. VDO.Ninja supplies hosted signaling and, when needed, relay servers; availability and service limits apply. Direct peers can learn each other’s network addresses.

Mic and camera start off. Leaving stops all captures. Protected streaming services may block picture or sound. Screen sharing adds network delay; you and your guest may see reactions slightly apart.

If something is quiet or stuck

Tap “Enable sound” if the browser blocked playback. Make sure BlackHole receives the movie, use headphones, and check the movie and voice volume sliders. If the connection fails after a network change, leave and re-open the same invite. Both people must use the same current app version.
