A QUICK GUIDE

A little setup.
A whole movie night.

Start with a movie file

On your Mac in Firefox, start a room, copy the invite, and choose a local movie file. MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio is a good first choice. Use the player’s play, pause, and seek controls. The file stays on your device; live picture and sound go to your guest. Files your browser cannot decode need VLC + screen sharing instead.

Share a movie playing on a Firefox site

  1. Install the free BlackHole 2ch driver. Restart your Mac if it is not listed in Audio MIDI Setup.
  2. In Audio MIDI Setup create a Multi-Output Device containing BlackHole and your headphones. Use the physical output as clock source and enable drift correction for BlackHole. Setup guide ↗
  3. In this app, click “Choose headphones” under Headphones / sound output and select the physical headphones or speakers, not System default, BlackHole or the Multi-Output Device. This keeps your guest’s voice out of the movie audio.
  4. Choose the Multi-Output Device as your Mac’s sound output. The movie tab now plays into your headphones and BlackHole. Other system sound can also be captured; keep unrelated apps quiet.
  5. Click “Find devices”, allow audio-input access, and select BlackHole 2ch under movie audio. Keep your real microphone selected under “Your microphone”. Leave “Listen to captured audio too” off, since you already hear the movie.
  6. Click “Share a window” and select the movie window. Allow Firefox screen recording in macOS settings if needed. Keep the movie and app tabs open.
  7. After movie night, restore your Mac’s sound output to your normal headphones.

For VLC, you can instead send VLC’s output directly to BlackHole while leaving macOS output on your headphones. Enable “Listen to captured audio too” if you cannot hear VLC locally. Only the host needs BlackHole. Local movie-file playback needs no driver.

One room, every movie night

Your invite does not expire when you leave. Share the guest invite once; you both can reuse your own links for future nights. The home page remembers your latest host room and guest seat separately in this browser. Keep a bookmark of your host link for another device or if browser data is cleared. “Create a different room” makes a separate invite; previous links still work. The host must be online and sharing for the guest to watch. Mic, camera, and screen sharing need to be started again each session.

Your guest

They open the invite in Chrome on Windows or Android and tap “Join movie night”. They can turn their microphone and camera on independently. Keep the browser open and the phone unlocked; backgrounding Chrome may interrupt playback or the call. On a phone, use landscape fullscreen for the movie.

Privacy & connection

One host and one guest are supported in this first version. Anyone with the full invite can join, so share it privately. Roles are for a trusted pair, not account authentication. Create a new room to get a new invite. Media is encrypted in transit using WebRTC. VDO.Ninja supplies hosted signaling and, when needed, relay servers; availability and service limits apply. Direct peers can learn each other’s network addresses.

Mic and camera start off. Leaving stops all captures. Protected streaming services may block picture or sound. Screen sharing adds network delay; you and your guest may see reactions slightly apart.

If something is quiet or stuck

Tap “Enable sound” if the browser blocked playback. Make sure BlackHole receives the movie, use headphones, and check the movie and voice volume sliders. If the connection fails after a network change, leave and re-open the same invite. Both people must use the same current app version.
